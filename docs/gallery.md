## Share your build

Built one? We would love to see it, even half-finished.
[Open a "share my build" issue](https://github.com/roykollensvendsen/garage-door/issues/new?template=share-a-build.yml)
with a photo or two, the opener model and anything you would do differently.
We will add it here and mark your opener as tested.

Please leave out anything private: house numbers, number plates, faces, and
your network's names or keys.
